House of Cards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

House of Cards ("we", "us", "our") operates the House of Cards website and mobile app (the "Service"), a marketplace and point-of-sale for trading cards, singles, slabs, and sealed products. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy. The Service is intended for users 18 years and older.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, username, email address, and mobile phone number when you create an account.
  • Orders & transactions — items you buy or sell, offers, order status, tracking numbers, and shipping or pickup details you provide.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment providers (Square, Stripe, and Afterpay). We do not store your full card numbers; we receive limited confirmation details such as amount, status, and a payment reference.
  • Content you upload — photos of items and any messages, reviews, or feedback you submit.
  • Notifications — a device notification token so we can send you alerts about orders, messages, offers, and shows.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical logs (such as app version and error information) used to keep the Service working.

How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and sign-in.
  • Process orders, offers, payments, refunds, and shipping.
  • Send you notifications you have enabled (orders, messages, offers, shows).
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • Show seller and buyer names, ratings, and feedback needed to run the marketplace.
  • Protect against fraud and keep the Service safe.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Payment providers — Square, Stripe, and Afterpay to process payments and refunds.
  • Hosting & backend — Supabase, which stores our data securely on our behalf.
  • Other users — when you buy or sell, the other party sees the name, ratings, and order details needed to complete the transaction.
  • Shipping — carriers you or the seller use to fulfill an order.
  • Legal — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and property.

Notifications

You can turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings or in your account within the app.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information. Payment card data is handled by our PCI-compliant payment providers and is not stored on our servers.

Your choices & rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or the deletion of your account, by contacting us using the details below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

The Service is not directed to children and is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact House of Cards through the Contact section of the app or at houseofcardsftwalton.com. Tell us if you'd like a support email address added here.
